Who we are

PennyBuys is the trading name of Netrue Limited, which is responsible for personal information collected through this website. For privacy questions or requests, email hello@pennybuys.co.uk.

Information we use

  • Customer account: your name, email address and a securely hashed password used to sign you in. Your sign-in session is stored in a protected browser cookie.
  • Newsletter: your email address and the date and record of your sign-up consent.
  • Product reviews: your account name, star rating, review text and submission and edit dates. Reviews appear publicly with your name.
  • Country and currency: if you opt in to an approximate location suggestion, we use your IP address through ipapi.co to identify your country from our supported list. Your country and currency preference is stored in your browser.
  • Basket: items and quantities are stored in your browser using local storage.
  • Installed app: the PennyBuys app caches its app shell and public product catalogue on this device for faster loading and basic offline browsing.
  • Orders: your name, email, phone number, delivery address, items, prices, order status and tracking number are stored in MongoDB so we can manage and display your order.
  • Support chat: your name, email if supplied, messages and message times are stored in MongoDB so our team can respond. A conversation ID is kept in your browser to reopen the chat.
  • Browser notifications: if you opt in, your browser push subscription and the email or conversation it relates to are stored in MongoDB to deliver order, support, arrival and store alerts. You can disable notifications in your browser settings.
  • Product administration: product and inventory information entered by our staff. Product photos and videos are stored using Cloudinary.

Please do not include sensitive personal information in a product review. Reviews are visible to other visitors.

How we use it

We use account details to provide sign-in and link reviews to the account that submitted them, so customers can manage their own reviews. We use newsletter details to send PennyBuys updates only when you have opted in. Product and inventory information is used to operate the catalogue.

We use order information to record and manage orders, show delivery progress and send status alerts when you have enabled browser notifications. Stripe processes checkout payments and tax calculations; PennyBuys does not receive or store full payment card details. Product prices can be displayed in GBP, NGN, BRL, QAR, AUD, USD or CAD. Non-GBP display prices use indicative daily GBP reference rates from Frankfurter; orders are charged and recorded in GBP.

Who receives it

Customer accounts, newsletter sign-ups, product reviews, orders, support conversations, messages and browser notification subscriptions are stored in our MongoDB database. Stripe receives the information needed to process checkout, verify delivery details and calculate tax. Authorised PennyBuys administrators can access order and support information to provide service, and review content and newsletter email addresses to manage the store and mailing list. Push notification delivery is handled through the browser platform provider associated with your device subscription. Railway hosts the website and Cloudinary stores product media. If you choose approximate location, ipapi.co receives the connection needed to return your country code. The browser requests reference exchange rates from Frankfurter; no customer details are sent in that request. These providers process information to provide their services to us.

We do not sell personal information. We may disclose it where required by law or to protect our rights and the security of the service.

How long we keep it

We keep newsletter information while you remain subscribed and remove or suppress it when you unsubscribe or ask us to delete it, subject to records we must retain by law. Product reviews remain published while relevant to the catalogue, unless removed or you request removal. Basket and chat-link information stored in your browser remains until you clear browser storage. We retain orders and support conversations while needed to provide service and handle related queries, then review and delete them when no longer needed, subject to records we must retain by law. Push subscriptions remain until you disable notifications, remove them in your browser, or the browser provider reports them as expired. We review retained information and delete it when it is no longer needed.

Your choices and rights

You can ask us to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal information, or object to its use where the law gives you that right. You can withdraw newsletter consent at any time on our unsubscribe page or by emailing us. Withdrawing consent does not affect earlier processing.

If you have a concern, please contact us first so we can help. You also have the right to complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ICO).

Security and changes

We use service providers and reasonable safeguards to protect information. No website or transmission can be guaranteed completely secure. We may update this notice as the website changes; the latest version and date will appear on this page.

Questions or privacy requests: contact us or email hello@pennybuys.co.uk.
