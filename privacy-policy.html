Who we are

PennyBuys is the trading name of Netrue Limited, which is responsible for personal information collected through this website. For privacy questions or requests, email hello@pennybuys.co.uk.

Information we use

  • Newsletter: your email address and the date and record of your sign-up consent.
  • Product reviews: the name you choose to display, your star rating, review text and submission date.
  • Shopping prototype: basket, demo account and demo order details stored in your browser using local storage.
  • Product administration: product and inventory information entered by our staff. Product photos and videos are stored using Cloudinary.

Please do not include sensitive personal information in a product review. Reviews are visible to other visitors.

How we use it

We use newsletter details to send PennyBuys updates only when you have opted in. We use reviews to show shoppers feedback about the relevant product. Product and inventory information is used to operate the catalogue.

This storefront currently provides a shopping demonstration. Basket, account and order information stays in your browser; the demo checkout does not transmit payment card details or place a live payment. The site does not currently use those demo details to fulfil real purchases.

Who receives it

Newsletter sign-ups and product reviews are stored in our MongoDB database. Authorised PennyBuys administrators can access newsletter email addresses to manage the mailing list. Railway hosts the website and Cloudinary stores product media. These providers process information to provide their services to us.

We do not sell personal information. We may disclose it where required by law or to protect our rights and the security of the service.

How long we keep it

We keep newsletter information while you remain subscribed and remove or suppress it when you unsubscribe or ask us to delete it, subject to records we must retain by law. Product reviews remain published while relevant to the catalogue, unless removed or you request removal. Browser-stored demo information remains until you clear your browser storage. We review retained information and delete it when it is no longer needed.

Your choices and rights

You can ask us to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal information, or object to its use where the law gives you that right. You can withdraw newsletter consent at any time by emailing us; include the email address you subscribed with. Withdrawing consent does not affect earlier processing.

If you have a concern, please contact us first so we can help. You also have the right to complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ICO).

Security and changes

We use service providers and reasonable safeguards to protect information. No website or transmission can be guaranteed completely secure. We may update this notice as the website changes; the latest version and date will appear on this page.

Questions or privacy requests: hello@pennybuys.co.uk.
